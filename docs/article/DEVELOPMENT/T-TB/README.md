# Scope T-TB — Theorem B, stability (v3 role: the HGED-relation record)

Originally the paper's central contribution — an explicit `C(k, Δ)` with
`d_I ≤ C(k, Δ) · HGED`. The completed analysis (T-TB, T-TBa, T-TBb) showed the
clean bound holds only under five hypotheses, two of which fail generically
(pointer-run drift in adversarial layouts; average span-boundedness
asymptotically), and D-ART2 (2026-07-18) drew the consequence: **Theorem B is
no longer a pillar.** What survives into the article is the closing
discussion's content — the length lemma `|w*_c| ≤ m(1+kn)` and the
unconditional envelope `d_I ≤ m(1+kn)·HGED` as propositions, the impossibility
of a bi-Lipschitz proxy for any complete invariant (the lower direction fails
for canonical/WL representations generically; our drift and avalanche
mechanisms kill the clean upper direction), and the three-regime coherence
prediction for the sensitivity histograms, now measured in the geometry pillar
(T-M5g). This scope keeps the analysis record (`theoretical/stability.md`
§2–4, the external proofs volume) and the remaining housekeeping/stretch
tasks; the conditional-bound program itself is follow-up work, not article
work.
